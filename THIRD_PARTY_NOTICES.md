# Third-party data and licensing notices

IvoireData does **not** relicense third-party datasets.

The Apache-2.0 license in `LICENSE` applies to the repository's original software code. Registry metadata and original documentation are intended for reuse under CC BY 4.0 unless a file states otherwise. Every external dataset keeps its original license, access conditions, attribution requirements and platform restrictions.

## Rights tiers

- **A_REDISTRIBUTABLE** — the source has a verified open license permitting redistribution/reuse, subject to the source terms and attribution requirements.
- **B_SHARE_ALIKE_OR_PLATFORM_TERMS** — open or reusable, but share-alike, access-gating, platform-only distribution, or similar obligations apply.
- **C_POINTER_ONLY_LICENSE_UNCLEAR** — publicly accessible material whose blanket redistribution/training rights were not verified. The registry stores metadata and a source pointer only.
- **D_RESEARCH_ONLY** — research/microdata access with restrictions; never included in the general-purpose training corpus by default.
- Other specific tiers document non-commercial, mixed, or source-specific constraints.

## Important examples

- `data.gouv.ci`: use under its Licence Ouverte, including attribution and last-update requirements.
- OpenStreetMap: ODbL obligations apply; do not mix an OSM-derived database into a differently licensed database without reviewing ODbL share-alike implications.
- Mozilla Common Voice: CC0 applies, but current Mozilla terms request that datasets be accessed through Mozilla Data Collective rather than mirrored elsewhere; this repository therefore keeps pointers/download helpers rather than copies.
- UVCI / Koumankan: follow the dataset card and CC-BY-SA/access-gating terms for each release.
- World Bank microdata: many microdata collections have research-only or licensed-access rules and are not redistributable here.
- Official public websites with no verified blanket open-data license (for example tax/legal/administrative portals): pointer-only until rights are clarified.

This file is operational guidance, not legal advice. Before releasing a model or commercial derivative, re-check the source terms in force at the time of use.
